It has a name

You're not imagining it.
All of it has a name.

Three changes that show up in the first year after surgery, what they're actually called, and how long each takes to turn over. Most people are never told any of this.

Telogen effluvium

Hair that sheds in handfuls

Rapid weight loss pushes a large share of follicles into their resting phase at once, so they shed together a few months later. It starts around month three. It usually passes.

Biotin 6,000 mcg, marine collagen, silicon, zinc, vitamin E — nutrients that support hair health.

Hair recovery: months, not weeks

Onychoschizia

Nails that split and peel

Layered splitting at the tip. Rapid weight loss and shifting nutrient status are common triggers — and nails turn over faster than hair, so they often show change first.

Silicon, biotin and zinc — nutrients that support nail strength.

A fingernail fully replaces itself in about six months

Xerosis

Skin that feels tight and dry

Dry, tight, flaky skin. Rapid weight loss, reduced intake and the fluid restrictions around meals are all common triggers.

Vitamin A 133% DV, vitamin E, zinc and vitamin C — the last two contribute to normal collagen formation.

Skin renews itself about every four weeks
